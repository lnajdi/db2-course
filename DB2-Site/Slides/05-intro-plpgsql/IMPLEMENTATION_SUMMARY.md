## 💡 Teaching Tips

### Maximizing the Enhancements:

1. **Checkpoint Slide:**
   - Actually stop and wait for everyone to complete
   - Walk around (or check screens if virtual)
   - Help stragglers before moving on

2. **Quiz Slide:**
   - Use as actual poll (raise hands, chat responses)
   - Discuss why wrong answers are wrong
   - Celebrate correct answers

3. **Troubleshooting Lab:**
   - Ask students what they think is wrong first
   - Share your own experiences with these errors
   - Make it collaborative, not just lecture

4. **Solutions Slides:**
   - Walk through step-by-step
   - Ask students to explain what each line does
   - Show alternative approaches
   - Use as code review practice

5. **Recap Slides:**
   - Invite questions specifically
   - Ask students what was most challenging
   - Preview next section to build anticipation
